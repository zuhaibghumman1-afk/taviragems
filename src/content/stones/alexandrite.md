## Overview

Alexandrite is a variety of chrysoberyl famous for its color change. In daylight it appears green to bluish green; under warm incandescent light it turns red to purplish red. The effect comes from chromium, which absorbs light in a way that lets the stone respond strongly to the light source.

## Origins

Alexandrite was discovered in Russia's Ural Mountains in the 1830s and named after the future Tsar Alexander II. The original Russian deposits are largely exhausted; Sri Lanka and Brazil are now the main sources. Fine stones with a strong color change are very rare.
