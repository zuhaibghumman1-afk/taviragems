## Overview

Topaz is one of the hardest silicate minerals, rated 8 on the Mohs scale. Natural topaz is often colorless or pale, while the most valued color is a golden orange to pinkish orange known as imperial topaz. Most blue topaz on the market is colorless topaz that has been treated to turn blue.

## Origins

Brazil, especially the area around Ouro Preto, is the classic source of imperial topaz. Topaz is also mined in Sri Lanka, Nigeria and Pakistan.
